# BENEFICIALai — 2-Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard
**Target Duration:** 2 Minutes (120 Seconds)  
**Hackathon:** DSH Hacks V2  
**Speaker Language:** English (or bilingual English + Roman Urdu)

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Hook & Real-World Problem
* **On Screen:** Show the BENEFICIALai landing page and statistics strip (*60%+ Medical Illiteracy, 45% Non-Adherence*).
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Every year, millions of chronic patients with Diabetes and Hypertension leave the clinic holding diagnostic lab reports filled with complex English jargon like HbA1c and eGFR that they simply cannot understand. In South Asia and developing communities, over 60% of patients cannot read their reports, and 45% fail to take their daily medicines properly — leading to preventable strokes, kidney failure, and emergency hospital readmissions. This is why we created **BENEFICIALai**: Intelligence That Benefits Life."*

---

### [0:25 - 0:55] Feature 1: Multimodal Lab Report Analyzer & Urdu Translation
* **On Screen:** Click "Load Demo Diabetic Report" or upload a lab sheet. Show the Yellow Triage banner, Roman Urdu explanation, and biomarker cards.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"With our Multimodal Diagnostic Analyzer, a patient simply uploads a photo or PDF of their blood test. Powered by Google Gemini AI, BENEFICIALai extracts key biomarkers, categorizes risk into Red, Yellow, or Green alerts, and explains everything in both English and clear Roman Urdu. For example, instead of intimidating numbers, the patient learns: 'Aapka HbA1c 8.4% hai, pichle 3 maah mein sugar control se bahar rahi hai.' It also suggests dietary cautions and exact questions to ask their doctor."*

---

### [0:55 - 1:25] Feature 2: Medication Companion & Adherence Tracking
* **On Screen:** Scroll to the Medication Companion. Show the active pill schedule. Click "Mark as Taken" on Januvia. Watch adherence jump to 75%.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Next is our Medication Companion. Managing 4 to 5 daily pills with different meal schedules is overwhelming. BENEFICIALai maps each medication to precise meal relations — like Metformin with breakfast or Atorvastatin after dinner. As the patient marks each dose, their real-time adherence score updates, gamifying consistency and preventing dangerous missed doses."*

---

### [1:25 - 1:45] Feature 3: Longitudinal Vitals & 1-Click Doctor Prep Report
* **On Screen:** Show the Sugar & BP Vitals Tracker. Then click the top-right button: "Doctor Visit Summary" modal pops up.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Patients can track their morning fasting glucose and BP over time. And when it’s time for their OPD appointment, one click generates a clean, printable Doctor Visit Summary with 7-day adherence metrics and longitudinal vitals — giving physicians instant clinical context in seconds."*

---

### [1:45 - 2:00] Conclusion & Real-World Impact
* **On Screen:** Back to the Hero header with the pastel pink floral aesthetic.
* **Voiceover:**  
  *"Built with React, FastAPI, and Gemini Multimodal AI, BENEFICIALai bridges the gap between complex diagnostic lab data and daily healthy action. Empowering chronic care patients, one life at a time. Thank you!"*
